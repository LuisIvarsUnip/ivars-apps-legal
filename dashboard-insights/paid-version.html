Dashboard Insights for Jira: the paid version

Dashboard Insights for Jira • Last updated: 2026-11-17

From 17 November 2026, new installations of Dashboard Insights for Jira use a paid version, sold and billed through the Atlassian Marketplace. This page explains what that means if you already use the app.

If you already have the app

  • Your installed version keeps working. Your charts and saved settings do not change on that date.
  • You will not be charged unless a Jira admin updates the app and subscribes.
  • You will see a short notice in the gadget. Each person can dismiss it. It does not affect your charts.

Our commitment

If we ever decide to limit or stop the free version, we will say so on this page and inside the app at least 30 days before it happens.

Updating to the new version

Updating is your Jira admin's choice.

  1. A Jira admin opens the manage apps screen and updates Dashboard Insights.
  2. The admin starts a trial or subscribes through Atlassian. This step is also needed for sites with up to 10 users, at no cost.

Your gadgets and saved settings stay in place after the update.

If a site updates and does not subscribe, the app keeps working for now. Before that changes, we will give the same 30 days of notice.

What you will see when you update

When a Jira admin clicks "Paid update", Atlassian shows a standard confirmation screen. Its wording is the same for every app, so here is what it means for this one.

  • "Free → Paid". The app changed from free to paid. It is the only change the screen lists.
  • "Updating the app will start a free trial. You will be charged after your trial period." This applies to sites with more than 10 Jira users, at the prices below. Sites with up to 10 users are in the free tier and are not charged.
  • "On update, you cannot use the free version of the app." After updating, a site cannot go back to the old version. If you do not update, nothing changes.
  • "If you keep it free, certain functionalities of the app will be limited." This is Atlassian's general wording. Our commitment above applies: the version you have keeps working, and we give 30 days of notice before that changes.

After updating, the subscription appears in Atlassian Administration, under Billing and subscriptions.
